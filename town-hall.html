Town Hall!

The Goldendale Creative District was born from the efforts of the Arts committee of the Goldendale Chamber of Commerce. The premise is simple - the well-being of our community is enhanced by artistic and creative pursuits, and the Goldendale economy can be driven forward through innovation, creativity and the arts.

Since our first town hall two years ago, the original Arts Committee has evolved into the Goldendale Creative District. We separated from the Chamber of Commerce and became a Washington charity with non-profit fiscal sponsorship provided by Shunpike Arts Collective. Our first arts project in 2025 was a mural at the baseball fields, and in 2026 we kicked off a series of events called the Four Seasons Creative Showcase.

We are actively looking for ways to engage more deeply with the Goldendale community, bring people together at events that celebrate the arts, and support local artists, creators and innovators. Come plan the future with us at our next town hall!
