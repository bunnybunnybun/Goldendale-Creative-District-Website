Goldendale Creative District is bringing people together to experience local arts, culture, restaurants and and lively gathering spots in downtown Goldendale.

Our upcoming events:

Make It and Take It Christmas Crafts!

We all love the winter holidays, but sometimes we can get a little cabin fever! Get out of the house and join us at The Lodge for a Winter Solstice Make and Take crafting event.

We’ll have fun activities for kids and adults. Come make last-minute Christmas cards, gift tags, ornaments for your tree and more! Bring your friends, your kids, your reluctant spouse, grandma and grandpa, and anyone else who wants to express their creative side this holiday season.

This is a fundraiser for the Creative District, but there is no charge to come enjoy the live music and refreshments. Everyone is welcome!

And a big thank you to The Lodge, our partner in this event!
