Baseball mural!

Baseball Mural, June 2025: A mural at the little league baseball field at Ekone Park was our first project as a Creative District. After putting out a call for artists, we selected one of the applicants, Scott Grey, and commissioned him to paint a mural on the storage container at the little league field. The mayor and the Chamber of Commerce joined us for a ribbon cutting ceremony for our first official installation.
